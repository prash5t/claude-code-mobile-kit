## Full description

> Build the habits that matter, one day at a time.
>
> DailyStreak helps you track simple daily habits and celebrates the streaks you build. Whether it's a daily walk, ten minutes of reading, or a morning gratitude practice, the app's job is to make showing up easy and the wins feel real.
>
> Features:
> • Track unlimited daily habits
> • Streak counters with milestone badges at 7, 30, 100, and 365 days
> • Beautiful, distraction-free interface
> • Works offline — your habits sync across devices when you sign in
> • Dark mode that follows your system
> • Optional home screen widget showing your current streak
>
> No accounts required to get started. Sign in optionally to sync between your phone and tablet.
>
> Privacy-first: we don't read the contents of your habits or share your data. Ads-only monetization keeps the app free without selling your information.
