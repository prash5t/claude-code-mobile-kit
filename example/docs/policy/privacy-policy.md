DailyStreak (the "App") is operated by the developer credited in the Google Play Store listing. This privacy policy describes what data the App collects, how it's used, and your rights.

---

## What we collect

### Automatically collected

- **Anonymous device identifier** (Firebase Installation ID): used to associate your habit data with your device without requiring you to sign in.
- **App usage events**: which screens you visit, when you log habits, when you reach milestones. Sent to Firebase Analytics. We do not collect the contents of your habits beyond their existence (we do not read your custom habit names for analytics purposes).
- **Crash diagnostics**: when the app crashes, technical information about the crash (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. No personal data is included.
- **Advertising identifiers** (Android Ad ID, iOS IDFA if granted): used by AdMob to serve ads. See AdMob's section below.

### Collected only if you opt in

- **Google account email**: collected only if you sign in with Google to back up your habit data across devices. Used solely to authenticate you and sync your data; not shared with third parties beyond Google Firebase Auth.

### Stored locally only (not sent to our servers)

- **Your habit list and habit logs**: stored in local on-device storage (Hive). Synced to Firebase Firestore only if you sign in with Google.

## Ads

We show banner ads from Google AdMob in a fixed area at the bottom of certain screens. We may also show occasional interstitial (full-screen) ads between habit-logging sessions. We do not show ads inside scrollable content, and we do not show interstitials on app launch.

AdMob may use your advertising identifier to serve more relevant ads. You can reset or limit ad tracking via:
- Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Reset advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
- iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

## Your rights

You can:

- **Request a copy of your data** by emailing the developer (contact in Play Store listing). If you've signed in with Google, your data is accessible via your habit history in the app.
- **Request deletion of your data**: delete the app to remove local data. If you've signed in with Google, also email the developer to delete cloud-stored data.
- **Opt out of analytics**: Settings → Privacy → "Help improve DailyStreak" toggle off. This stops new analytics events from being sent.

For users in the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA), or India (DPDP): you have specific rights under those regulations. Contact the developer to exercise them.
